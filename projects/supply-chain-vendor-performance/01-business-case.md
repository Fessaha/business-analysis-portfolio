# Supply Chain Optimization & Vendor Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This is a simulated Business Analysis case study for a fictional Swiss trading company, SwissTrade AG.

The project focuses on analysing delivery delays, supplier performance, and opportunities to improve the procurement and supply-chain process.

## Business Problem

SwissTrade AG is experiencing recurring delivery delays and limited visibility into supplier performance. Products are not always available when required, employees need to follow up with suppliers repeatedly, and management lacks a centralised view of supplier reliability and delivery performance.

## Business Impact

The delivery issues are associated with increased logistics costs, reduced product availability, additional operational effort, and declining customer satisfaction.

For the purpose of this simulated case study, logistics costs are assumed to have increased by 15% over the last two quarters.

## Initial Hypotheses

Potential contributing factors include:

- Supplier SLA non-compliance
- Inefficient inventory management

These factors are initial hypotheses and will be validated through process and data analysis rather than assumed to be root causes.

## Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Identify the main factors contributing to delivery delays
- Evaluate supplier performance using measurable KPIs
- Identify suppliers that consistently meet or fail agreed service expectations
- Identify opportunities to improve the procurement and supply-chain process
- Provide management with a Power BI dashboard for data-driven supplier performance monitoring and decision-making
