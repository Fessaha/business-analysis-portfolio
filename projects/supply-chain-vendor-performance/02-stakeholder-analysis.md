# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify the key stakeholders affected by the supply-chain and supplier-performance issues at SwissTrade AG, understand their needs, and determine how they should be engaged during the project.

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Main Interest / Need | Influence | Interest | Engagement Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Management | Visibility into supplier performance, costs, delays, and risks to support decision-making | High | High | Manage closely |
| Procurement | Identify reliable suppliers and improve purchasing decisions | High | High | Manage closely |
| Warehouse / Inventory | Visibility into stock levels and expected deliveries to maintain appropriate inventory levels | Low | High | Keep informed |
| Suppliers / Vendors | Clear order quantities, delivery dates, and agreed service requirements | High | Low | Keep satisfied |
| Customers | Product availability and delivery within the promised timeframe | Low | High | Keep informed |
| Logistics / Transport | Visibility into what needs to be transported and when deliveries are expected | Low* | Low* | Monitor |

*The influence and interest of Logistics will be reviewed as the analysis progresses because its role in delivery delays has not yet been established.

## Key Observation

Stakeholder needs differ across the supply chain. Management and Procurement require performance information for decision-making, while Warehouse, Customers, Suppliers, and Logistics are primarily concerned with product availability, order accuracy, and reliable delivery.

These stakeholder needs will be used as inputs for the next stages of the analysis, including process analysis, business questions, requirements, and KPI definition.
