# AS-IS Process Analysis

## Purpose

The purpose of the AS-IS analysis is to understand the current procurement and delivery process at SwissTrade AG and identify key pain points that may contribute to delivery delays and operational inefficiencies.

## Current Process

1. A need to replenish a product is identified.
2. Procurement creates and sends a purchase order to the supplier.
3. The supplier confirms the order and provides a promised delivery date.
4. The supplier prepares the ordered products.
5. The products are handed over to the logistics/transport provider.
6. The logistics provider transports the products.
7. The products arrive at the warehouse.
8. The actual delivery date is recorded and can be compared with the promised delivery date.

## Key Pain Points

### 1. Limited Order Tracking

After an order has been placed, SwissTrade has limited visibility into its current status. Delivery problems may therefore be identified too late, reducing the ability of Procurement and Warehouse staff to react proactively.

### 2. Lack of Supplier Performance Measurement

SwissTrade does not systematically track which suppliers deliver on time and which suppliers frequently miss promised delivery dates. Procurement therefore lacks reliable performance information when selecting and evaluating suppliers.

### 3. Product Availability Issues

Delivery delays can lead to insufficient product availability in the warehouse. This may prevent customer orders from being fulfilled on time and negatively affect customer satisfaction.

## Current-State Business Impact

The identified pain points can result in:

- Additional follow-up work for Procurement
- Reduced visibility across the supply chain
- Delayed identification of delivery problems
- Product availability issues
- Potential customer dissatisfaction

## Analysis Note

The causes of delivery delays have not yet been confirmed. Supplier performance, logistics performance, inventory management, and other potential contributing factors will be investigated during later process and data analysis.
